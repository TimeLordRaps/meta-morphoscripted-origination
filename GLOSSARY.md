## This repository's words

**morphoscript, morphoscripted.** From the repository's name and its
description, *"The scripture that morphs to morph scriptures"*, and from
Tyler's sentence *"This will be our story morphoscripted from origin to
progination."* The word is not in Tyler's lexicon, and no meaning beyond those
two uses is assumed here.

**origination.** Used in its plain English sense: the coming into being of
something, or the point where it begins. *Collision:* unpublished work in
progress in a sibling repository uses the same word, in a different sense, for
the continuing creation of a realm. The two senses should not be merged
without a decision.

**progination.** Tyler's word, used only in his phrase *"from origin to
progination"*. It is not in his lexicon, and its precise meaning is waiting on
him. Until then, nothing in this repository defines it.

**origination declaration.** One file in [declarations/](declarations/README.md):
an origin story for one emotion, idea, belief, piece of knowledge or thought,
in the shape set by [the schema](schema/origination-declaration.schema.json).
The Origination Declarations at the top of the [README](README.md) are the
repository's own opening statement, in Tyler's voice. *Collision:* on its own,
"declaration" is a working term in several sibling repositories (see the table
below), so here it appears only in this two-word phrase.

**global religion surface.** Tyler's phrase for what the declarations add up
to. See [SURFACE.md](SURFACE.md). *Collision:* "surface" appears in `hypermath`
in other senses. Here it names the map as a whole and is not a formal object.

**anchor point.** From the description: *"anchor points for religions to pass
through"*. [PLACEMENT.md](PLACEMENT.md) §2 reads it formally as a bottleneck
every path passes through, a *dominator*, and §3 records that "anchor" already
carries other senses in sibling repositories. Here it is a display name only.

**frame.** From the description: *"what frame is discoverable from limited
cognition"*. PLACEMENT.md §3 finds it already carries conflicting senses in
sibling repositories. Here it is used only in ordinary prose.

**morph.** One stage in a declaration's `morphs` list: a shape the thing took
at a certain time. *Soft collision:* mathematics uses "-morphism" for a map
that preserves structure (homomorphism, isomorphism). A morph here preserves
nothing in particular. It is only a stage.

**the five kinds.** Emotion, idea, belief, knowledge and thought, as Tyler
listed them. These drafts sort by the following working rules, which are
sorting aids and not definitions:

- **emotion**: a feeling, known from what people did because of it;
- **idea**: a concept that can be passed on, such as a sign standing for something;
- **belief**: something held true about the world or about others, often before it can be checked;
- **knowledge**: something that can be checked against the world, and works;
- **thought**: a way of thinking, a habit of mind.

**tenet link.** A place where a declaration's subject lives in a present-day
tradition, described in that tradition's own terms, with a reference to its own
texts or practices. Tenet links are always listed alphabetically by tradition.

**paradox-held.** A tension that people hold on both sides at once, without
resolving it. From Tyler's correction recorded in [SCOPE.md](SCOPE.md):
*"contradictions can be held paradoxically by people"*. A declaration's
`paradox_held` list keeps these, because a plain overlap of what traditions
hold would drop them.

**singularitian.** Tyler's word, from the directed goal: *"singularitians like
myself"*. It is close to the more common "singularitarian". See also
*singularitaken*, above.

**consensus, speculation, confidence.** In a declaration, `consensus` lists
what most specialists would accept and `speculation` lists what is proposed but
not settled. They are always kept apart. `confidence` is one of `high`,
`medium`, `low` or `speculative`, and says how firmly a particular claim or
source stands, not how important it is.
